12 / FAQ

Questions,
answered.

A few quick answers before you place your order.

How do I order?+

Choose a product, click Order Now, enter your Fortnite/Epic username, then use Copy Order Information and join the GameNest WhatsApp group.

Do I need an account?+

You only need the Fortnite/Epic username requested by the order form. Payment and order completion are handled with the GameNest admin.

Where do I send my order information?+

After joining the GameNest WhatsApp group, text the GameNest admin and send the copied order information.

Do I pay on the website?+

No. The website does not process payment. Payment is handled with the GameNest admin after you join the WhatsApp group.

What payment methods are available?+

GameNest lists Vodafone Cash, WE Pay, InstaPay and Telda. The admin will assist you with the payment process after you join the group.

How does the WhatsApp process work?+

Open the group from your order modal, join it, then text the GameNest admin with your copied order information so they can assist with your order.
