06 / FAQ

Questions? Answered.

How do I order?

Choose a product, click Order Now, then use Copy Order Information or WhatsApp to contact GameNest.

How fast is delivery?

Orders are processed after payment confirmation. Delivery timing can vary, so contact GameNest for the current estimate.

What payment methods are supported?

GameNest supports Vodafone Cash, WE Pay, InstaPay and Telda.

Can I order V-Bucks?

Yes. Choose one of the V-Bucks packages shown in the store.

Can I order Fortnite Crew?

Yes. Select the Crew duration you want and press Order.

How do I contact support?

Use the WhatsApp button in the order window or the Contact section.
