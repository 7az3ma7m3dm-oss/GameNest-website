How do I place an order?+

Choose a product, select a package, then use the order window to contact GameNest with your selection.

Are prices in EGP?+

Yes. The storefront prices are displayed in Egyptian pounds (EGP).

Can I ask questions first?+

Yes. Confirm the package, payment method and current order details with GameNest before paying.

Is GameNest affiliated with Epic Games?+

No. GameNest is an independent storefront and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Epic Games.
