SMALL PRINT

Questions,
answered.

Everything people ask before their first ticket.

How do I actually order?+

Pick a pack, tap Open ticket, add your Fortnite/Epic username, then use Copy Ticket and join the GameNest WhatsApp group to send it.

Do I need to create an account?+

No. Only your Fortnite/Epic username goes on the ticket. The admin handles the rest of the order in chat.

Where does my ticket actually go?+

Nowhere until you send it — copying it doesn't submit anything. You paste it yourself to the GameNest admin inside the WhatsApp group.

Can I pay on the website?+

No, and that's intentional — this site never asks for payment details. Payment is arranged directly with the admin after you join the group.

Which payment methods work?+

Vodafone Cash, WE Pay, InstaPay and Telda. The admin will guide you through whichever one you use.

What if I never got a reply?+

Keep your copied ticket text — it has your order number on it — and message the admin again in the group with the same ticket.
